Basic Information
Used in the header shown to participants and in the export file name.
Used to signal the response language to AI features. Does not translate the interface.
The description is also used by the AI attribute suggestion on the next page, so the more detail you provide here, the better the suggestions will be.
Attributes & Levels

Define 1–6 attributes. Each attribute needs 2–4 levels. Drag to reorder attributes or levels within an attribute.

Attributes have different numbers of levels. Designs with equal numbers of levels per attribute are preferred for generating research design suggestions.
